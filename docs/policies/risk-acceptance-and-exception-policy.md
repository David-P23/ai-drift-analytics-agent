# NorthStar Risk Acceptance and Exception Policy

> Synthetic demonstration document. It is included to demonstrate retrieval-grounded agent behavior and is not a real company policy or legal advice.

## Exception Eligibility

An exception request must identify the affected application, current version state, business justification, compensating controls, accountable Business Owner, Technology Owner, and requested expiry date. An exception is not approval to ignore remediation; it is a time-bound risk decision.

## Approval and Expiry

Mission Critical exceptions require joint approval by the accountable Business Owner and the governance authority. Pending requests do not change the risk posture. Denied requests require a remediation plan or formal escalation. No exception may extend beyond the documented review date without renewed evidence and approval.

## Monitoring Requirements

Open exceptions are reviewed at least monthly. Exceptions attached to 90-day or 120-day drift findings receive the same aging escalation as the underlying finding. The Technology Owner records remediation progress; the Business Owner confirms whether residual business risk remains acceptable.
