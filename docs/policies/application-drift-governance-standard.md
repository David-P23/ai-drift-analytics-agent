# NorthStar Application Drift Governance Standard

> Synthetic demonstration document. It represents a portfolio governance pattern and is not a real company policy, legal advice, or a statement of regulatory compliance.

## Control Objective

NorthStar maintains an approved version baseline for in-scope applications. A version difference is recorded as drift and remains open until remediation is verified, an approved exception is active, or the application is removed from scope through documented governance review.

## Ownership and Escalation

The Technology Owner initiates remediation and maintains technical evidence. The Business Owner is accountable for impact acceptance and prioritization. Findings on Mission Critical applications require a remediation plan within two business days. Findings aged 90 days require senior-management review; findings aged 120 days require executive governance review.

## Evidence and Review Cadence

Each material decision must retain the approved baseline, detected version, risk tier, owner, decision rationale, and review date. Governance reviews occur monthly, with ad hoc review when a 120-day finding, denied exception, or high-concentration drift wave is identified.
