# NorthStar 2026 Remediation Initiative

> Synthetic demonstration document. It illustrates a realistic operational initiative for the portfolio demo and does not describe a real organization or current regulatory mandate.

## Initiative Goal

The 2026 initiative reduces aging application drift through risk-based sequencing: resolve Mission Critical exposure first, remove repeated product-family drift through shared remediation waves, and close pending exception decisions before they become governance escalations.

## Operating Priorities

Teams prioritize findings using criticality, days open, concentration by product or data center, and exception state. A data center with concentrated drift should receive a coordinated remediation review rather than isolated application-by-application action. Repeated findings in a product family should trigger root-cause analysis of the deployment, inventory, or baseline-management process.

## Success Measures

The initiative tracks Mission Critical open drift, 90-day and 120-day aging volume, pending-exception aging, remediation cycle time, and recurrence by product family. Leaders use these measures to decide where to assign accountable owners and where to sponsor a cross-team remediation wave.
